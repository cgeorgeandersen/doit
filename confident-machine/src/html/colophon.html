About this essay

How it was made

This essay was researched, written and built with the help of an AI assistant, working from a human editor’s brief. That is fitting, and a little awkward. It is also why every factual claim links to a primary source: you shouldn’t have to take a confident machine’s word for anything, including this one’s.

The quotes, figures and dates were checked against their sources. Where a claim could not be verified, it was rewritten or cut. The hiring town in Chapter 5 is synthetic, and says so.

How facts are dated

Every time-sensitive claim lives in one file with its source, the date it describes and the date it was last checked. The small stamps beside those claims show the check date. After 120 days without a check, a stamp turns amber and reads “may be out of date”. The page header shows when this copy was built.

Claims labelled Contested are ones where careful researchers disagree. They are shown side by side, not averaged.

What runs in your browser

Both models on this page, the word predictor in Chapter 1 and the hiring screener in Chapter 5, are trained in your browser, from scratch, when you reach them. Nothing you type or choose is sent anywhere. Your case file is saved only in this browser, and you can clear it from the case file panel.
