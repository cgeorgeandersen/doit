    How the numbers work

    Your hours

    Each answer becomes a weekly number: under 2h counts as 1, 2 to 5h as 3.5, 5 to 10h as 7.5 and 10h+ as 12. The hours are split across the steps by how long each kind of step usually takes. The step you said drags gets two and a half times its usual share.

    The four labels

    Every kind of step has a label, based on the four questions: is it chasing or waiting, is it the same every time, is it reading or writing, or does it need a person.

    Time you could get back

    Each kind of step has a best case, like 80% of the time spent scheduling or 5% of the time spent in real conversations. Nobody gets the best case everywhere, so the range assumes you get 35% to 65% of it. For you that's hours a week.

    Your type

    Your type is the label you have more of than a typical week does, compared across all 21 workflows. It describes your week, not you.
