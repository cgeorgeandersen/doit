The method

Fix first. AI last.

Most "we need AI" problems are really process problems or plain automation. Every task climbs the same ladder, and we stop at the cheapest rung that works.

Rung 1Fix the process

Chasing, rework, waiting. Remove the cause before buying a tool.

Rung 2Automate it

Same steps every time? Rules and integrations. No AI needed.

Rung 3Augment with AI

Reading, writing, summarizing. AI drafts, you decide.

Rung 4Keep it human

Judgment, trust, taste. Protect this time.
