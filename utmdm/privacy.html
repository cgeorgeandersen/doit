How TagFluent handles your data

TagFluent is a tool for classifying marketing UTMs. This page says what it collects, where that is kept, who can see it, and how to have it deleted. The short version: we collect what the product needs to work, we keep it in Amazon Web Services in the United States, and we never sell it or use it for advertising.

Last updated October 8, 2026

Who we are

TagFluent (tagfluent.com) is operated by George Andersen. Questions about this page or your data go to cgeorgeandersen@outlook.com.

What we collect

Your account
Your email address and password, kept by Amazon Cognito, the sign-in service TagFluent uses. Cognito stores passwords in a form no one can read back, including us. New accounts are reviewed before they open, so when you sign up we receive an email with your address.
Your workspace
The UTMs you add, the columns and rules you create, the values you type, the name you give yourself, and the history of every change. This is the product, so it is kept until you ask us to delete it.
Demo requests
If you book a demo, the name, work email, company, team and message you enter are emailed to us. They are not stored in TagFluent's database.
Things your browser remembers
Your browser keeps your sign-in session, your light or dark choice, and your column widths, so you don't have to set them again. TagFluent uses no advertising or tracking cookies and no third-party analytics.
Error logs
Our servers keep basic error logs for 30 days so we can fix problems.

Google Analytics

You can choose to connect a Google Analytics 4 property to import your UTMs. When you do, Google asks you to allow read-only access to your Analytics data (the analytics.readonly permission). TagFluent then reads the list of properties you can see and, for the property and dates you pick, the campaign source, medium, name, content and term of your sessions with session and key event counts. It does not read anything about individual visitors.

The access Google grants stays in your browser tab for about an hour and is never sent to or stored on TagFluent's servers. Only the UTMs you choose to add are saved, to your workspace. TagFluent cannot change anything in Google Analytics. You can remove its access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

TagFluent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google data only to show and import your UTMs, never for advertising, never to train AI models, and we don't let anyone read it except as needed for security, to comply with the law, or with your permission.

How we use it

To run the product: to sign you in, save and show your workspace, import your UTMs, answer your demo request and support questions, and keep the service secure. We may email you about your account or your demo request. We will not add you to a marketing list without asking.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use your workspace or Google data to train AI models.

Where it's kept and who can see it

Everything is kept in Amazon Web Services in the United States (Ohio). Your workspace is stored in Amazon DynamoDB, encrypted at rest, with continuous backups kept for 35 days. Everything travels over HTTPS. Each workspace is tied to its own account, and only that account can read or change it through TagFluent.

We use these service providers, which handle data only to provide their service to us:

  • Amazon Web Services: hosting, sign-in (Cognito), storage (DynamoDB) and notification emails.
  • Google: only if you connect Google Analytics, under Google's own privacy policy.

We share data with no one else, except when the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your account and workspace are kept while your account exists. You can download a full backup of your workspace at any time from Import & export, and clear it with Start with an empty table. To delete your account and everything in it, email us from the address you signed up with. We delete it within 30 days, and it leaves our backups within a further 35 days. Demo request emails are deleted when they are no longer needed to follow up.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete the personal data we hold about you, and to object to how we use it. Email us and we will answer within 30 days. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

TagFluent is a business tool and is not meant for anyone under 16. We don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change how TagFluent handles data, we will update this page and its date, and for significant changes we will email account holders before they take effect.
