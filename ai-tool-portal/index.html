What these tools take for granted

All three are built on these. If you disagree with one, the tools will disagree with you in the same place.

“Don't build it” is a good outcome

A lot of work needs a rule, a report someone already built, or a licensed feature nobody switched on. Ruling out an idea in fifteen minutes is cheaper than finding out after a quarter.

Ordinary automation often beats AI

When the rules are written down and the inputs are clean, a workflow or scheduled job gives the same answer every time and leaves an audit trail. A model is non-deterministic by design.

Hours saved are not money saved

Freed time with nowhere to go gets absorbed. The case desk counts it at zero until someone commits it to named work, a backfill that won't happen, or overtime that stops.

Every case uses the same yardstick

One confidence haircut, one adoption ramp, one discount rate, one five-year window. Councils then compare cases on the same terms, whichever team was most optimistic.
