Where each item can land

rank distribution across the simulation

Every score in this backlog is an estimate with a tier attached, and the eleven bottlers who voted are a sample of the ones who could have. The simulation redraws both — perturbing each score by its tier's uncertainty and resampling voters with replacement — then re-ranks from scratch. A dark band means the item lands in that rank almost every time; a smeared band means its position is an artefact of noise, not a finding.

Regime map

who is #1 as the blend and the weights move

Sweeping one parameter from 0 to 1 and re-ranking at each step shows the regions where the answer does not change. If your current setting sits in the middle of a wide band, the meeting about that weight is not worth having. If it sits near an edge, the ranking is a coin-flip on a number nobody measured.

Each cell is Kendall's τ-b: of all the pairs of backlog items two bottlers both ranked, the share they ordered the same way, scaled to run from −1 to +1.
